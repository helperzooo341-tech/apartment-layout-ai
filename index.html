🏠 Apartment Layout AI

AI tool for analyzing apartment layouts and generating space optimization ideas for real estate professionals and buyers.

What this tool does

  • Analyzes apartment layouts
  • Suggests furniture placement
  • Generates real estate descriptions
  • Provides space optimization ideas

Example input

2-room apartment, 54 m², separate kitchen, balcony

Example output

Optimized layout with open living space, storage near entrance, and functional zoning between kitchen and living area.
